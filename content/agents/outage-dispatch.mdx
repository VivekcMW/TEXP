## Where it fits

Distribution utilities and energy operators where dispatchers read tickets by hand and the process collapses under storm volume.

## What changes for the team

The intended change is a review queue with related reports and incident evidence brought together. Dispatch suitability, escalation handling and any operational benefit require validation.

## Where it fits

Multi-site manufacturers where planners spend their day reading work orders and looking up assets before the real work starts.

## What changes for the team

Planners can review evidence-backed proposals rather than assembling each one from scratch. Safety handling, review effort and audit suitability must be assessed against site requirements.

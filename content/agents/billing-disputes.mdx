## Where it fits

Operators with high dispute volume and billing systems inherited from mergers, where consistency of credits is as big a problem as speed.

## What changes for the team

Cited explanations and recorded credit reasoning can support more consistent review. People retain judgement calls; the effect on service quality should be evaluated against representative disputes.

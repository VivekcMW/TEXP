## Where it fits

Provider groups and hospital departments where coordinators spend hours per request and clinicians review after hours.

## What changes for the team

The intended change is an evidence-linked draft for clinician review, with missing criteria made visible before submission. Review effort and payer outcomes depend on the case and must be evaluated separately.

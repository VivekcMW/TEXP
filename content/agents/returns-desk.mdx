## Where it fits

Retailers and marketplaces with a support queue dominated by returns, refunds and order status.

## What changes for the team

Support teams can focus on judgement cases while routine lookups and draft replies follow agreed rules. Consistency, handling effort and escalation quality need evaluation using your own cases.

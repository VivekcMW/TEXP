## Where it fits

Carriers, insurers and 3PLs with a claims desk that grows with volume. The agent takes the reading, lookup and drafting; the handlers keep the judgement calls.

## What changes for the team

The intended change is a queue of evidence-linked drafts for review rather than repeated inbox and system lookups. Handling effort and escalation quality need evaluation in your process.

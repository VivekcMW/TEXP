## Where the cost hides

Operations: onboarding files, reconciliation breaks, complaints. All of it document-heavy, all of it measured in analyst hours.

## What we have built

Reconciliation and claims agents on the same pattern as our logistics work: read, evidence, draft, escalate. The case study linked shows the architecture.

## Compliance notes

Every agent ships with the scope, evaluation and monitoring documentation your model risk team needs. Decisions that regulation reserves for people stay with people.

## Where the cost hides

The support queue after a launch or a sale, and the spreadsheet work behind merchandising. Both scale with volume, and volume is seasonal.

## What we have built

A returns assistant that resolves the majority of tickets with a cited policy answer, and reconciliation agents for supplier files.

## Compliance notes

Card data never enters the model path. Refund decisions above a threshold are always made by a person, and the assistant says so.

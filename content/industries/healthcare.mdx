## Where the cost hides

Administrative work around care: authorisations, documentation, coding, referrals. Staff spend hours on it and clinicians spend evenings on it.

## What we have built

A prior-authorisation agent that assembles the evidence and drafts the request for a clinician to sign, cutting drafting time from days to minutes.

## Compliance notes

PHI never leaves your environment. Every draft carries its sources. Nothing is submitted without a named person's approval.

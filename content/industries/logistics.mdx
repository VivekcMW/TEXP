## Where the cost hides

Claims, exceptions and documents. None of them is a product; all of them are headcount. A mid-size carrier typically has thirty people whose day is reading one system and typing into another.

## What we have built

Claims agents that read, evidence and draft decisions; exception agents that triage and route; document agents that extract and reconcile. Each shipped behind a human gate first.

## Compliance notes

Customs data stays in its region. Every automated decision carries the passages and records it was based on, so an auditor can replay it.

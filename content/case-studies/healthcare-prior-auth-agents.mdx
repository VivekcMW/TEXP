## Situation

Two days of elapsed time and an hour of coordinator effort per request, with clinicians reviewing after hours.

## What was built

An agent with read access to the EHR and the payer criteria library, a drafting tool that links every statement to a source, and a signature queue. The agent cannot submit.

## Result

Drafting time fell from two days to nine minutes of agent time plus a short clinician review. First-pass approval rate rose because the evidence was complete.

## What we would do differently

Build the payer criteria library as a maintained asset from day one. Payer rules changed twice during the pilot and the first update was manual.

## What to evaluate

Review evidence completeness, citation accuracy, missing-criteria handling and clinician corrections. Submission quality and payer decisions are separate outcomes.

## Requirements to confirm

Confirm encounter-level access, data residency, contractual requirements, payer-rule maintenance and the clinician signature workflow before implementation.

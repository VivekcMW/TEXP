## Situation

Nine thousand tickets a month, inconsistent answers, and a seasonal queue that forced temporary hiring.

## What was built

Permission-aware retrieval over policies and the catalogue, an assistant that cites every answer, and an evaluation harness that runs on real tickets weekly.

## Result

Seven in ten tickets resolved without a person; accuracy on the evaluation set above the agreed bar for twelve consecutive weeks before autonomy was widened.

## What we would do differently

Involve the legal team in the evaluation set from week one. Their edge cases were the ones that mattered, and they were added in week four.

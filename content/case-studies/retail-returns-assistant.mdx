## What to evaluate

Review answer accuracy, policy consistency, escalation quality and the effort required to resolve exceptions. Test unclear eligibility and conflicting order records as well as routine returns.

## Requirements to confirm

Agree source ownership, refund limits, approval rules and how policy updates reach the assistant. Legal and support teams should review representative edge cases.

## What to evaluate

Test evidence quality, proposed priorities, planner corrections and safety escalation against representative site cases. Do not treat a recommendation as permission to carry out work.

## Requirements to confirm

Agree read-only boundaries, safety responsibilities, historian access and the approvals required for schedule or assignment changes.

## Situation

Four days per claim, three systems, one spreadsheet, and a desk that grew with volume. The baseline was measured over four weeks before anything was built.

## What was built

A single agent with four tools: read email and attachments, query the TMS, draft a decision, and escalate. The escalation rules were written with the claims lead and changed twice during the pilot.

## Result

Median cycle time fell from four days to six hours. Handler time per claim fell from forty minutes to four, mostly spent reviewing escalations.

## What we would do differently

Start the photo-evidence tooling earlier. Image-based damage assessment was the last piece to reach the confidence bar and held back full autonomy on a third of claims.

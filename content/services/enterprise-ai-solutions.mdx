## Who this is for

CIOs and transformation leaders at organisations with more than one AI initiative and no shared foundation.

## What we build

A gateway, retrieval layer, identity and logging that every team uses; the governance that lets them use it without a committee; and three workflows that prove it works.

## What you get after sixteen weeks

An inventory of every model call, a cost line per department, and teams shipping on a foundation your security team has already approved.

## Who this is for

Department heads with a KPI and a budget who have outgrown generic tools.

## What we build

An agent whose job is written down as a contract, a tool layer that enforces it, and an evaluation suite that proves it before anyone relies on it.

## What you get after ten weeks

An agent handling real volume behind a human gate, metrics that show when to widen its autonomy, and a team that can change it without fear.

## Who this is for

Support, HR, legal and operations leaders whose teams answer the same questions from the same documents every day.

## What we build

A pipeline that turns your documents into retrievable passages, an assistant that answers only from those passages and shows its sources, and an evaluation harness that tells you every week whether quality moved.

## What you get after six weeks

An assistant your team trusts because it cites, a dashboard that shows resolution rate and answer accuracy, and a refresh process that keeps it current as documents change.

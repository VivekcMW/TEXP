## Who this is for

COOs, CFOs and transformation leads who need to pick the first AI pilot and defend the choice.

## What we build

A register of candidate processes with the numbers behind each one, a readiness assessment that names the blockers, and a plan the board can approve in one meeting.

## What you get after three weeks

Clarity on where the money is, what would stall you, and a pilot proposal with a price and a measurable target.
